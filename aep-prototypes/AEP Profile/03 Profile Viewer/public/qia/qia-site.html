QIA in Focus

QIA was established in 2005 to protect and grow Qatar’s financial assets, and to help diversify the economy.

QIA is a global investment organization, with investments spanning all major global markets, asset classes, sectors and geographies.

We are a diverse organization with over 66 nationalities in our teams.

We aim to have a positive impact through all our investments.

QIA is contributing to the Qatar National Vision.
