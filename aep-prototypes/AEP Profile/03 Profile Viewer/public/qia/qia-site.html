02 · Deployment plan

Campaign foundation first, personalisation next

A phased plan reflecting the call: newsletter and mailing-list orchestration is the demonstrable starting point. AEM integration and Target personalisation are described as future opportunities, not live capabilities.

Foundation

Agree the campaign operating model

Define subscriber types, mailing purposes, channel ownership, measures and approval workflow.

Governance

Consent taxonomy

Purpose, source, timestamp, withdrawal and audit fields.

Content

Newsletter model

Topics, languages, cadence and reusable modules.

Measurement

Campaign baseline

Delivery, engagement and unsubscribe measures.

Data readiness

Unify subscriber preferences

Map consented mailing-list and behavioural signals into governed profiles without assuming access to unapproved personal data.

Platform

Subscriber schema

Type, topics, locale, consent and activity events.

Identity

Approved stitching

Email or CRM keys with transparent merge rules.

Audience

Reusable segments

Interest plus consent, with suppression policy.

Pilot

Activate a governed newsletter

Select one subscriber audience and campaign, orchestrate delivery, and inspect profile and engagement events in the Lab.

Journey

Email activation

Eligibility, frequency cap, send and fallback.

Profile

Response signals

Delivery, open, click and preference updates.

Insight

Learning loop

Audience and content performance review.

Planned opportunity

Connect AEM, then introduce Target

AEM-managed public content can become reusable campaign and web experience content. Once Target is deployed and governance is agreed, consented segments could support on-site testing and personalisation.
