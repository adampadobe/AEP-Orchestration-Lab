  • On November 28, 2022, HRH Crown Prince Mohammed Bin Salman bin Abdulaziz announced the development of King Salman International Airport (KSIA). This ambitious project aims to support Saudi Arabia's aspirations by establishing a world-class aviation hub for international, regional, and domestic travel.

  • King Salman International Airport will be located to the North of Riyadh on an extended area adjacent to the existing King Khaled International Airport, covering approximately 57km² with six runways and six terminals.

  • KSIA is a symbol of Riyadh's ambitions and a key driver for the city's transformation into a global hub for business, tourism, and culture — meeting future demand as tourism booms under Vision 2030.

  • KSIA will serve up to 100 million travelers by 2030 and up to 185 million passengers by 2050, driving growth in airport services, logistics, and local employment.
