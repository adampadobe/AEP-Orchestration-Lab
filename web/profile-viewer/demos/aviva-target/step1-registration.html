Your eligibility

Please check you are eligible for Motor insurance with Aviva

  • You are a permanent resident of the United Kingdom, the Isle of Man or the Channel Islands.
  • All vehicles being insured under this policy are registered and kept at your home or business address in the United Kingdom, the Isle of Man or Channel Islands.
  • You and all drivers have advised the DVLA or DVA or any notifiable illnesses or disabilities and they have agreed to the issue of a licence.
  • No driver has previously been refused insurance or had a policy cancelled.
  • You or any named driver do not have one of the following occupations: Professional entertainer, footballer, boxer, gambler, chauffeur, taxi driver, driving instructor or parcel/takeaway/fast food delivery.

Electric Vehicle Benefits

Included in our Comprehensive Cover

Recovery when you're out of charge

If your electric vehicle runs out of charge, an RAC mechanic will come out and either charge your battery to get you to a charging point, or transport you, your vehicle and up to seven passengers to the nearest working charging point, or your home. Whichever's closer.

Charging equipment cover

We cover accessories such as charging cables ( in your vehicle and while in use), as well as wall box chargers installed in your garage.

Protection from accidental damage

We'll be there for you if your car's lost or damaged, including if its damaged by electrical surge while charging.

Lifetime repair guarantee

Following a claim we guarantee the quality of repairs made by our approved repairers for as long as you own the car, even if you change insurers.

Battery cover

Electric car batteries are covered for accidental damage, fire, and theft as part of your car.

How do you use your van?

Social, domestic & pleasure only

This covers you for normal day to day driving, such as a trip to the shops or the school run. You are not covered for travelling to and from work or for any business use.

Covers social, domestic and pleasure. Also covers any named driver(s) driving to and from a single place of work in any one day.

What type of cover would you like?

Third party, fire and theft covers you for losses as a result of fire or theft but not for collision damage to your vehicle.

Please note third party, fire and theft cover is not available where there are additional drivers under 25 years old or your vehicle is under 10 years old.

Comprehensive cover provides the same cover as third party, fire and theft, plus cover for accidental damage to the vehicle you're insuring.

FAQs

A no claim discount (NCD) is money taken off your premium to recognise that you haven't made a claim for a year or more, and you're the main driver on a UK motor insurance policy. Your no claim discount could increase with each consecutive year you don't claim.

You may also qualify if you're a named driver on another Aviva motor policy or you've earned a no claim discount driving in another country. Contact us (opens in a new tab or window) to find out.

You can only use your no claim discount on one vehicle at a time.

A no claim discount is valid for 2 years after your last motor insurance policy expires. If you haven't been a main driver on a policy for over 2 years, your no claim discount will no longer be valid.

Please see our motor insurance FAQs (opens in a new tab or window) for more information on no claim discount if you require more information before getting a quote.

When you buy a motor insurance policy, you may get the option to protect your no claim discount (NCD) for an additional cost. Protecting your discount means you can make a claim without losing any of your discount at renewal, even if you were at fault. Your premium may change at renewal, but it won't increase as a direct result of one claim.
