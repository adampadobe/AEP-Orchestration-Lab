The future of computing

Rene Haas in conversation with industry leaders on AI, industry trends, and the compute platform shift reshaping cloud infrastructure.

As agentic AI systems move from experimentation to production, the conversation has shifted from model size to system orchestration. CPUs remain the coordination layer for accelerators, memory, networking, and control-plane software across the rack.

Arm CEO Rene Haas discusses how hyperscalers and cloud providers are re-architecting data centers for sustained AI throughput, lower power draw, and predictable total cost of ownership — and why Neoverse is becoming the default substrate for cloud-native AI infrastructure.

Three themes from the discussion

  • Efficiency at rack scale — performance per watt determines how many agentic workloads a facility can sustain without expanding power envelopes.
  • Software-defined infrastructure — developers need consistent toolchains from edge to cloud; Arm Performix and Neoverse documentation accelerate migration.
  • Partner ecosystem velocity — NVIDIA, AWS, Google Cloud, and Microsoft Azure are shipping Arm-based AI platforms for inference and orchestration.
