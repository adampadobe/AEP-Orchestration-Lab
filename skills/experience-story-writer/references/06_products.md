Last updated: April 2026 (Summit 2026 update)
How to feature each Adobe product specifically in a story — never describe capabilities generically, always show them doing a real thing.
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════

RULE: Every product must appear at a moment where it does something specific and impressive. Show it, don't describe it.

New entries: Adobe CX Enterprise (the unified surface concept), Adobe Brand Intelligence, NVIDIA 3D Digital Twins, AI Collaborators / Content Reviewer in Workfront, Content Authenticity for Enterprises (CAFE), GenStudio Content Marketing Insights. Updated entries: Brand Concierge (with new Commerce/AJO/Semantic Profile/Knowledge Agent integrations), AEM Sites and AEM Assets (with new agent names), AEM Sites Optimizer (with pre-flight recommendations), AJO and RTCDP (with Summit-expanded skills), CJA and Data Insights Agent (with V2 capabilities and MCP connectivity), Marketing Campaign Analytics (replacing Mix Modeller in some contexts), GenStudio for Performance Marketing (now with self-service trial GA).

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════

CRITICAL: Adobe CX Enterprise is the flagship name introduced at Summit 2026. It is the surface, not a product to buy. — Don't describe Adobe CX Enterprise abstractly — always show it as the place where work is happening
