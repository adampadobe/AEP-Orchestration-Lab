WHAT IS NEW IN THIS PLAY POST-SUMMIT
The following Summit 2026 announcements are directly relevant to CSC stories. Use them when the story brief calls for them.

— Adobe Brand Intelligence (Q2 2026): the world's first fine-tuned LLM with integrated vision-language capabilities, purpose-built to scale creative production, validation, and performance of enterprise content. Anchored in a dynamic brand ontology that continuously ingests and updates brand data — brand evolves from static snapshot to living, adaptive system. Encodes brand identity (guidelines, tone, design system, visual standards) and enforces it across every piece of content generated by humans or agents. Augments human brand stewardship rather than replacing reviewers — handles high-volume repeatable compliance checks so reviewers focus on judgment calls. Trained on a brand's own assets, guidelines, historical content, combined with the Brand Context Protocol (BCP).

— NVIDIA Foundry and 3D Digital Twins (Q2 2026): strategic partnership delivering the world's first cloud-native, identity-preserving 3D digital twin platform for marketing content production. "Identity-preserving" means accurate, brand-consistent visual fidelity to the physical product (materials, lighting response, proportions). Critical for automotive, beauty, fashion, consumer electronics. Digital twin assets flow directly into GenStudio workflows.

— GenStudio Content Marketing Insights (Q2 2026): part of the new GenStudio Content Marketing module. Helps brands and publishers assemble, analyse, and optimise content marketing performance. Content analytics inside the content production workflow.

— GenStudio for Performance Marketing — Self-Service Trials (GA): now available as a self-service trial. Sign up with Adobe ID, no sales-assisted onboarding.

— AI Collaborators in Workfront (Content Reviewer GA at Summit; additional agent invocation H2 2026): a new way for AI agents to show up inside Workfront — not as a conversational assistant but as a permissioned team member that can be assigned work. Content Reviewer is the first out-of-the-box AI Collaborator. Distinct from AI Assistant (conversational), agents (purpose-built specialists), and CX Enterprise Coworker (orchestrates multiple agents toward a goal). AI Collaborators operate as permissioned participants inside defined workflows.

— Content Authenticity for Enterprises (CAFE): brings Content Credentials into core creative and marketing workflows across Content Supply Chain, AJO, AEM, Firefly Services. Operationalises transparency, provenance, trust across the content lifecycle. Built on C2PA open standards. Enterprise-grade additions: certificate-backed signing, tamper-resistant metadata, interoperability across platforms. CAFE available now; credentials in CXO Fall 2026.

— Adobe CX Enterprise Coworker (announced, GA H2 2026): a persistent, goal-oriented super-agent that orchestrates multiple purpose-built agents toward a business outcome. Has memory, can be invoked manually, triggered by a signal, or run on a schedule. Always ask the consultant before featuring Coworker in a marketer story; default is to feature Coworker, with the consultant able to opt out for individual purpose-built agents.
