NEW FLAGSHIP NAME
Adobe is now positioning Customer Experience Orchestration under a single flagship name: Adobe CX Enterprise. It replaces "Experience Cloud" plus the creative solutions required to deliver the full CXO vision. Adobe CX Enterprise is the surface marketers work in. It is not a new SKU. Existing investments are protected. The pillars of Adobe CX Enterprise are Brand Visibility, Customer Engagement, and Content Supply Chain — running on Adobe AI Platform with Agent Orchestrator at the core.

GTM stories typically express a B2B framing of Customer Engagement (account-based and buying-group journeys) and increasingly Brand Visibility (B2B buyers researching vendors via LLMs).

NARRATIVE SHIFT
Adobe is leading with outcomes, not architecture. For GTM stories specifically, the new outcome language is "buyers engaged through the full buying group with role-specific dynamically assembled content" rather than "lead-to-revenue funnel optimisation."

WHAT IS NEW IN THIS PLAY POST-SUMMIT
The Field Readiness Guide is heavily B2C-weighted; B2B updates are referenced primarily through session listings rather than the announcement body. The following items apply to GTM stories.

— Audience Agent (B2B) and Journey Agent (B2B) via AJO B2B Edition: same agentic acceleration as the B2C versions, applied to account-based and lead-based B2B journeys. — B2B applicability of LLMO and GEO: B2B buyers increasingly research vendors via LLMs. Brands not structured for LLM readability risk being invisible or misrepresented in AI-generated answers. GTM stories should consider Brand Visibility moments where the B2B buyer first encounters the vendor through an AI answer rather than a website. LLM Optimizer with Verified Business Impact (H1 2026) brings A/B-style measurement to GEO actions.

— Adobe CX Enterprise Coworker (announced, GA H2 2026): a persistent super-agent that orchestrates multiple agents toward a goal. In B2B contexts, particularly powerful for orchestrating across long, multi-stakeholder buying cycles. Always ask the consultant before featuring Coworker in a marketer story; default is to feature Coworker, with the consultant able to opt out for individual purpose-built agents.

TARGET MOTION
B2B organisations. Designed for leadership teams — typically C-suite and VP level. Strategic conversation starter with a goal of supporting transformational deals. Use in CECs or when bringing new executive buyers into strategic conversations.

PLAY DEFINITION
Adobe Experience Cloud is a single platform to transform GTM strategies for B2B organisations by aligning Marketing and Sales data, content, journeys, and commerce powered by new AI innovations.

VALUE PROPOSITION
Adobe makes organisations agentic-ready so they can transform a highly manual, resource-intensive process into a cross-functional orchestration of GTM operations. Marketing Ops will own a wave of AI adoption, using Adobe as a command centre to plan, create, engage, and optimise buyer journeys. Sales can plug in to those journeys with insights and use new Adobe agents for prospecting and customer research. Adobe uses GenAI to accelerate content authoring and design for teams in Demand Gen, Web, and Commerce. — Accelerates revenue through unified GTM orchestration: aligning marketing and sales data, content, and journeys on a single platform shortens deal cycles and improves conversion.

Use cases:
— Orchestrate marketing and sales efforts: coordinate advisor outreach and marketing campaigns for complex products (commercial loans, treasury services) using unified data and AI agents.
— Accelerate demand gen impact: deploy intelligent tools to automate compliance-heavy campaign workflows for wealth management or institutional banking.
— Drive content-led growth: deliver personalised digital experiences for credit solutions or embedded finance offerings.

Proof points:

DIMENSION 1: INITIATIVES & CHALLENGES

Questions:
— Tell me more about your upcoming priorities.
— Are any of your top priorities connected to data or cloud initiatives?
— What compelling event/external pressures/internal pressures have encouraged you to focus on these priorities?
— What are you doing today to achieve these priorities?
— Are you achieving these priorities? What specific scenarios would get you closer?
— What is keeping you from achieving your priorities?
— Why do you think you are running into these problems?
— What solutions have you tried? Where did those fall short?
— What will happen if these issues go unresolved?

What to listen for (initiatives): AI adoption as CEO/board-level mandate, modernisation/migration/cloud/tech stack consolidation, strengthening enterprise trust and thought leadership, aligning brand/demand/product/partner/customer marketing into one revenue-aligned engine, simplifying complexity of global marketing and sales organisation.

What to listen for (challenges): Very little AI today, struggle trusting model efficacy; technology and data fragmented across functions; islands of automation, not an integrated GTM engine; buyer journey spans digital/product/field sales/post-sale but messaging not consistently connected; board wants predictable revenue and efficient growth in a market with long cycles and high scrutiny.
